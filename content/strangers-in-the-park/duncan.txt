Lost in the Desert
When I was sixteen years old, over the course of five days, I was lost in the desert after a night hike gone wrong. Over those five days I survived, for the first couple days off of Analgin and a bag of quick oats and the company of my best friend. During that time, we faced a lot of trials, conflict… mainly the will to keep going even though you don't know where the end is. Rescue helicopters flew over us and went right past us. Trains would pass. We would try going to people's houses and no one seemed to be home in this vast empty desert. We eventually had to lean on each other, continuing to press forward, trying to find the will to take one more step. Oftentimes we didn't have that will, but thankfully the other did and vice versa. This concept of constantly pushing forward to keep going no matter what because you're not done yet. Because even though we were young, 16 and 18, we didn't know quite where our life was going. We knew that we wanted to do great things, that we wanted to help other people. It was a lesson of friendship, but also how to rely on yourself and to push forward, even though the next day isn't guaranteed.
