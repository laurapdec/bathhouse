The Bar Exam
So I actually just became a lawyer, but before that I had to take the bar exam not once but twice.
The first time I studied for the bar exam, I spent all summer studying basically like waking up super early and then going to bed at like unreasonable times to do my best to get a passing score.
Unfortunately, the first time I took it I only missed by a couple points, which was really frustrating.
It was also additionally weird because I took the California bar exam as did a celebrity you may or may not know, Kim Kardashian. So, I kind of went on this whole mental spiral about like my own self-worth in the process.
No shade to Kim Kardashian. I hope that she does eventually pass the bar sooner than later.
But yeah, the second time I took the bar, the stakes were higher because I was already employed and I was scared about, you know, I felt like my job was riding on this and I had a lot less time to study. But, I made it work.
I got up early, stayed up late, tried to focus on the things that confused me the last time.
And through a combo of good luck and a lot of other things, I did end up passing the February one. And I'm now a lawyer, which is a very strange sentence to say to people when they ask me what I do now. But, yeah, the California Bar is harder than the New York State Bar, so I was kind of nervous because in law school I wasn't always like the best student. But I really wanted to work in California and wanted to do something different. And I think this was my sign to keep pushing forward, even if like it was a harder road to get there.
