Three Times I Stood Up
September 10th, 2021. My supernatural experience. I was shot in the chest by a coworker at my job. As I retreated, I happened to, I fell, I passed-- I basically passed out. I was on the floor and all of a sudden in my head I hear “GET UPPPPP!” in my ear spook me that I scared, and I rose. I looked around, there was no one there. I kept on walking, stumbling as I walk, I fell again, a block over, and I heard the voice come again. I got up. I kept on going. I made it in front of El Dorado bumper cars in Coney Island, and I fell again, and the voice came again.
About seven months before that, I had a dream that I got shot. And in that dream I saw a billboard and I was laying on the floor and I saw the ambulance coming.
Back to the day of me getting shot, after I rose the third time, I walked and I made it to Stillwell Avenue where I saw the billboard and I laid down. And that's where my help came. That's my supernatural story.
