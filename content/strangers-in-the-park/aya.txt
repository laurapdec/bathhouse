The Annabelle Fear
So when I was younger, I was probably 7 or 8, I was the most nervous person of anyone ever. I saw the Annabelle commercial for the movie and it scared me so bad that I had to sleep on my parents couch in their bedroom for months. I couldn't fall asleep naturally every night. I would lay facing the door. I would wake up with a crick in my neck and I would fall asleep with my eyes open just so that I-- in case anything came in, I would always be prepared. And after a while, my parents were like, you need to move on. Go back to your room. So I did what any six year old would do, 7 years old. And I crashed on my brothers’ bedroom floor for their entire seventh grade year. I just slept on the floor with a blanket and a pillow and my stuffed animal. And they had to wake up at 6am for middle school every morning. And so at 6am their alarm would go off and all three of us would groan and roll around and have to get ready for school. And it was, you know, how alarms get stuck in your head and it's like the most egregious sound ever. And so then a couple years later, my dad made that sound his ringtone for his phone. He's a doctor, so he gets calls every five minutes. And every time it would go off, everybody in the house would, oh my god, just groan cause it's the most PTSD noise ever. And I just think it's beautiful how my family, we all have the same experiences. We grew up in the same house, but the one thing that really gives us the strongest emotional reaction is the sound of a ringtone. And it's just so striking. And I'm still that nervous person, but I think I'm a little less nervous now, so…
