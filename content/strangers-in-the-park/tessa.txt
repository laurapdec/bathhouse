Moving to New York at Fifteen
I guess I'm going to tell the story of how I moved to New York City.
I moved here when I was 15 years old by myself. I'm originally from Texas, and if anyone knows anything about Texas, it's a very small place. Like, big state, small place.
And I came here for dance. I applied to be a part of this dance program, Joffrey Ballet School. And I didn't tell anyone when I applied.
So I remember, I was 15 years old in Austin, Texas. I remember I was actually in my, like world history class in freshman year of high school. And I was like, I have to make the decision to pursue what I want. Like I have to be brave about this. And I cannot think small.
So I applied to Joffrey Ballet School and I got in with a full scholarship, and like I didn't tell my parents. I didn't ask anyone's permission. I just kind of did it. And it was a really adult thing to do at a really young age.
And for some miraculous reason, my parents said yes. And they were like, "We're gonna let you do it."
And so I packed up my stuff, and at 15, I moved here. And it was this weird adult world. Like I had to take care of myself, and I had to make things happen. And I realized that caring about your passions and your dreams does not just mean like, "Oh, I love it, so I'm gonna go do it."
It also means you need to have the passion to support yourself too. And you need to have the passion to take care of yourself. And like the passion for responsibility is also a really big deal when it comes to your creative passions and things you wanna do with your life.
So yeah, I'm now in my—I'm going into my fourth year at Joffrey. And I feel like it's this big full-circle moment of like, I came here not knowing anything, and at least at the end of it, I can say I figured something out. Like I figured out how to live here. And I feel like I've learned so much at such a young age. And I'm so grateful for that knowledge.
